# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mexi Cuts customers, primarily mobile users, who need to book and manage local haircut appointments without calling or exchanging messages. The barber also relies on the same booking data to keep the schedule accurate.

## Product Purpose

Make booking, confirming, rescheduling, and cancelling a Mexi Cuts appointment quick and trustworthy. Success means customers can manage upcoming appointments themselves, receive useful notifications, and never create double-bookings or stale availability.

## Brand Personality

Bold, youthful, and playful. The experience uses Mexi Cuts' established retro pixel identity while keeping transactional actions direct and familiar.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic corporate scheduling platform, an over-polished luxury salon site, or a cluttered marketplace such as Booksy. Avoid decorative effects that obscure dates, prices, availability, or booking actions.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action obvious on a phone.
2. Keep booking state accurate across every customer and admin surface.
3. Let customers recover from changed plans without contacting the barber.
4. Use personality in presentation, not at the expense of clarity.
5. Confirm consequential actions and explain failures in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, zoom-friendly layouts, and reduced-motion-safe behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate booking status or action meaning.
